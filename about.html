Photographer and designer based in New Hampshire. Five years shooting product, portrait, and brand work, with a BA in Graphic Design & Web Design from SNHU. Self-taught behind the camera, formally trained at the desk — one person running the whole pipeline, from image to identity to live site.

Because the photography and the design come from the same hands, every project ships as one consistent look — the shoot and the layout that carries it never feel like two different people.
